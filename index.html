Conference Scope

Where Materials Science Meets Clean Energy

This conference on nanomaterials, hydrogen and advanced energy materials and devices will provide participants with insights into the design, synthesis, and characterization of materials used in modern energy systems such as batteries, fuel cells, and solar devices.

It will also highlight recent advancements and research opportunities in areas like nanomaterials, energy storage, and sustainable technologies. Overall, the conference aims to bridge the gap between fundamental research and real-world energy applications, preparing participants for future innovations in the energy sector.

Thematic Areas of Conference

Four Pillars of Future Energy Research

Original research abstracts are invited across all four thematic tracks of ICNHAEM-2027.

TRACK 01
grain

Nanomaterials for Energy and Functional Applications

  • Synthesis and design of nanostructured materials (nanoparticles, nanowires, nanosheets)
  • 2D materials (graphene, MXenes, TMDs, silicene) and their heterostructures
  • Nanocomposites and hybrid nanostructures for energy applications
  • Surface engineering, doping, and defect functionalization of materials
TRACK 02
water_drop

Hydrogen Generation, Storage and Utilization

  • Electrocatalytic and photocatalytic water splitting for hydrogen production
  • Metal hydrides, complex hydrides, and MOF/porous materials for hydrogen storage
  • Hydrogen adsorption/desorption kinetics, thermodynamics and reversibility
  • Fuel cells and hydrogen-based electrochemical energy conversion devices
TRACK 03
battery_charging_full

Advanced Energy Materials and Storage Devices

  • Lithium-ion, sodium-ion, potassium-ion and beyond-lithium battery technologies
  • Supercapacitors, hybrid capacitors and high-power energy storage systems
  • Solid-state electrolyte and next-generation battery architectures
  • Electrode materials design, interface engineering and performance optimization
TRACK 04
eco

Emerging and Sustainable Energy Technologies

  • Nanomaterials for photovoltaics, thermoelectric and energy harvesting
  • Redox flow batteries and grid-scale energy storage solutions
  • Green synthesis routes and eco-friendly/sustainable energy materials
  • Integration of nanomaterials in flexible, wearable and miniaturized energy devices

In Memoriam

This Conference is Dedicated to Padma Shri Prof. O. N. Srivastava

Prof. O. N. Srivastava was an Indian material physicist, an Emeritus Professor of Banaras Hindu University and the Vice President for India and South Asia of the International Association for Hydrogen Energy, who was known for his contributions to the disciplines of nanotechnology and hydrogen energy. He was the author of two books and over 440 scientific papers and a recipient of several honors including the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize, the highest Indian award in the science and technology categories. The Government of India awarded him the fourth highest civilian honor of the Padma Shri in 2016 for his contributions to science and engineering.
