About the conference

Where materials science meets energy

ICNHAEM-2027 offers insights into the design, synthesis and characterisation of materials used in modern energy systems — from batteries and fuel cells to solar devices.

The conference highlights recent advances and research opportunities in nanomaterials, hydrogen generation and storage, and sustainable energy technologies. It is designed to bridge fundamental research with real-world energy applications and prepare participants for the next wave of innovation in the energy sector.

Thematic areas

Four themes, one energy future

Submissions are invited across the full spectrum of nanomaterials, hydrogen and advanced energy materials and devices.

THEME 01
grain

Nanomaterials for Energy & Functional Applications

  • Synthesis and design of nanoparticles, nanowires and nanosheets
  • 2D materials — graphene, MXenes, TMDs, silicene — and heterostructures
  • Nanocomposites and hybrid nanostructures for energy applications
  • Surface engineering, doping and defect functionalisation
THEME 02
water_drop

Hydrogen Generation, Storage & Utilisation

  • Electrocatalytic and photocatalytic water splitting
  • Metal hydrides, complex hydrides and MOF/porous materials
  • Adsorption/desorption kinetics, thermodynamics and reversibility
  • Fuel cells and hydrogen-based electrochemical devices
THEME 03
battery_full

Advanced Energy Materials & Storage Devices

  • Li-ion, Na-ion, K-ion and beyond-lithium batteries
  • Supercapacitors, hybrid capacitors and high-power systems
  • Solid-state electrolytes and next-generation architectures
  • Electrode design, interface engineering and performance optimisation
THEME 04
eco

Emerging & Sustainable Energy Technologies

  • Nanomaterials for photovoltaics, thermoelectrics and energy harvesting
  • Redox flow batteries and grid-scale storage solutions
  • Green synthesis routes and eco-friendly energy materials
  • Flexible, wearable and miniaturised energy devices

Dedicated to Padma Shri Prof. O. N. Srivastava

31.12.1942 – 24.04.2021

An Indian materials physicist, Emeritus Professor at Banaras Hindu University and vice president for India and South Asia of the International Association for Hydrogen Energy. He authored two books and over 440 scientific papers, received the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize, and was awarded the Padma Shri in 2016 for his contributions to science and engineering.
