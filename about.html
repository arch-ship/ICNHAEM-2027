About the conference

ICNHAEM-2027 at Banaras Hindu University — a forum for nanomaterials, hydrogen and advanced energy materials and devices.

Our purpose

Bridging research and real-world energy

This conference on nanomaterials, hydrogen and advanced energy materials and devices gives participants insights into the design, synthesis and characterisation of materials used in modern energy systems such as batteries, fuel cells and solar devices.

It highlights recent advancements and research opportunities in nanomaterials, energy storage and sustainable technologies. The intent is to connect fundamental research with practical energy applications and to prepare participants for future innovations in the energy sector.

This conference is dedicated to Padma Shri Prof. O. N. Srivastava

31.12.1942 – 24.04.2021

Prof. Onkar Nath Srivastava was an Indian materials physicist, an Emeritus Professor of Banaras Hindu University and the vice president for India and South Asia of the International Association for Hydrogen Energy. He was known for his contributions to nanotechnology and hydrogen energy, authored two books and over 440 scientific papers, and received several honours including the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize — the highest Indian award in science and technology. The Government of India awarded him the Padma Shri in 2016 for his contributions to science and engineering.

Thematic areas

What the conference covers

Four connected themes spanning synthesis, characterisation, storage devices and sustainable energy systems.

01 / NANOMATERIALS
grain

Nanomaterials for Energy and Functional Applications

  • Synthesis and design of nanostructured materials — nanoparticles, nanowires, nanosheets
  • 2D materials: graphene, MXenes, TMDs, silicene and their heterostructures
  • Nanocomposites and hybrid nanostructures for energy applications
  • Surface engineering, doping and defect functionalisation of materials
02 / HYDROGEN
water_drop

Hydrogen Generation, Storage and Utilisation

  • Electrocatalytic and photocatalytic water splitting for hydrogen production
  • Metal hydrides, complex hydrides and MOF/porous materials for hydrogen storage
  • Hydrogen adsorption/desorption kinetics, thermodynamics and reversibility
  • Fuel cells and hydrogen-based electrochemical energy conversion devices
03 / ENERGY STORAGE
battery_charging_full

Advanced Energy Materials and Storage Devices

  • Lithium-ion, sodium-ion, potassium-ion and beyond-lithium battery technologies
  • Supercapacitors, hybrid capacitors and high-power energy storage systems
  • Solid-state electrolytes and next-generation battery architectures
  • Electrode materials design, interface engineering and performance optimisation
04 / SUSTAINABILITY
eco

Emerging and Sustainable Energy Technologies

  • Nanomaterials for photovoltaics, thermoelectric and energy harvesting
  • Redox flow batteries and grid-scale energy storage solutions
  • Green synthesis routes and eco-friendly/sustainable energy materials
  • Integration of nanomaterials in flexible, wearable and miniaturised energy devices
